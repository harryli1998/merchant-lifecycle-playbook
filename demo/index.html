Read these numbers as a worked evaluation method. The generator contains pre-stop warning behavior. Performance on this data is not evidence of performance on real merchants.

Inspect lifecycle definitions and label stability

States use account age, spend change and active days. Value tier is separate: a small stable account is still Stable. Onboarding precedes history checks; current zero-spend is Inactive; then At-risk, Growth, Stable, and Observe.

Frequent changes may reflect genuine behavior or brittle rules. Pooled transitions reuse accounts and mix different periods; stability alone is not validity. Review account trajectories and operator feedback before rollout.

04 / CLOSE THE LOOP

A diagnosis becomes
a testable decision.

Account labels support a conversation. The intervention still needs its own evidence.

  1. 01

    Agree the decision

    Define activation, repeat behavior, value, operating cadence and team capacity.

  2. 02

    Check the inputs

    Reconcile account IDs, dates, currencies and missing observations. Review samples with operators.

  3. 03

    Run a scoped pilot

    Randomize eligible accounts within stage; log assignment, attempted contact, exposure and costs.

  4. 04

    Measure incrementality

    Compare outcomes by original assignment, with uncertainty and guardrails. A better label is not uplift.
