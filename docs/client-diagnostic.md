# Scoping a merchant lifecycle diagnostic

## A useful starting question

“Which merchants need help becoming repeat advertisers, and how should our small team prioritize support?”

Fit: advertising platforms or marketplaces with repeated account activity, accessible historical records, and an owner able to act on results. This prototype is less informative when purchases are inherently infrequent, data cannot distinguish missing from zero, or no operational team can implement a pilot.

## What to share first

Platform type; the difficult operating decision; account and campaign definitions; available history; number of people who can act; current success metrics and known seasonality. Share descriptions or an anonymized schema initially, not customer records.

## Scoped outputs

1. Data-readiness and metric-definition memo, including gaps and exclusions.
2. Cohort/segment diagnostic with denominators and competing explanations.
3. Explainable rules, versioned thresholds, and samples for operator review.
4. Capacity-aware action list with reasons, required checks and suggested motions.
5. Walkthrough and intervention measurement plan.

Deliverable scope and timing follow the readiness review. This does not include rebuilding an ad server, automatic outreach, production CRM integration, guaranteed uplift or a full experimentation platform.

## What would make the analysis stronger

Campaign start/end dates, budget caps, advertiser performance/ROI, category seasonality, acquisition cost/channel, support tickets, account-owner context, prior outreach and costs. These distinguish completed campaigns and planned pauses from avoidable friction. Behavioral decline alone does not reveal the cause.

## How to assess value

Before implementation, agree the decision the diagnostic must change and the feasible test. After implementation, measure incremental contribution profit or defined revenue per eligible randomized account; include contact and incentive costs. Keep descriptive shifts separate from causal claims. A threshold tradeoff can be valuable without a sophisticated predictive model.
